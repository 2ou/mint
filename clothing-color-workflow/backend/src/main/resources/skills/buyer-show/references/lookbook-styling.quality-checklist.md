# Quality Checklist

Check every final image before delivery.

## Fidelity

- All locked garment colors, prints, construction details, lengths, and fabric behavior are preserved.
- Locked model identity remains recognizable; the reference outfit is preserved only when requested.
- Added styling supports the garment and does not hide selling details.

## Composition and Scene

- Canvas is 3:4 vertical unless the user requested another ratio.
- Category-safe framing is respected; no important garment area, hands, feet, or footwear is cropped.
- The subject has believable ground contact, shadows, perspective, and depth in the scene.
- Image-set shots are distinct but visually coherent.

## Realism

- Skin, hair, textiles, and environment have natural texture.
- Lighting direction, reflected color, and shadows agree across the subject and scene.
- Anatomy, hands, footwear, layering, and garment drape are plausible.

## Avoid

- altered garment, missing trim, wrong print, altered color, or accidental extra layers
- plastic skin, beauty-filter look, mannequin stiffness, distorted hands, duplicate limbs
- logos, watermarks, illegible text, or copied interface elements
