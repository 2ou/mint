# Lookbook Recipes

Use a single visual story across an image set. Start with a hero frame, then add supporting frames that reveal different product details without repeating the same pose.

## Urban Everyday

- **Mood:** confident, approachable, contemporary.
- **Settings:** sidewalk, cafe exterior, bookshop, subway entrance, gallery district.
- **Shot rhythm:** full-body walking hero -> three-quarter detail at a storefront -> seated or leaning lifestyle frame.
- **Light:** soft morning side light or late-afternoon city glow.

## Cabin and Autumn Outdoor

- **Mood:** warm, relaxed, tactile, weekend escape.
- **Settings:** wooden steps, cabin porch, forest path, lakeside dock.
- **Shot rhythm:** full-body stair hero -> side view showing layers -> close detail of hem, fabric, footwear, or bag.
- **Light:** golden-hour side or back light with believable contact shadows.

## Resort and Vacation

- **Mood:** effortless, sun-warmed, aspirational but natural.
- **Settings:** terrace, coastal path, poolside, linen-draped cabana.
- **Shot rhythm:** full-body movement -> waist-up relaxed portrait -> environmental wide shot.
- **Light:** clear natural daylight or sunset; use wind only when the garment benefits from motion.

## Minimal Editorial Studio

- **Mood:** clean, product-led, polished.
- **Settings:** textured neutral backdrop, soft seamless paper, one architectural prop at most.
- **Shot rhythm:** front full-body hero -> profile or back construction view -> close material detail.
- **Light:** one directional soft key with controlled shadow; retain realistic texture.

## Set Variety Rules

- Keep the same garment facts across every shot.
- Change at least two of pose, camera height, scene position, light direction, or product detail focus between adjacent shots.
- Reserve the hero frame for the clearest product read; use supporting frames for fabric, trim, movement, or styling context.
