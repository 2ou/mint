# 镜头语言参考指南

## 镜头距离（Shot Size）

### 极特写（Extreme Close-up, ECU）
**描述**：画面只包含面部某个局部或产品细节
**应用**：
- 眼睛特写：突出眼神、情感
- 嘴唇特写：展示口红、表情
- 配饰特写：珠宝、手表、纽扣
- 面料特写：纹理、编织细节

**提示词**：
```
Extreme close-up of [眼睛/嘴唇/配饰/面料], 
ultra-detailed, sharp focus, 
[光线描述], [质感描述]
```

**示例**：
```
Extreme close-up of woman's eyes with natural makeup, 
eyelashes in sharp focus, soft light reflecting in iris, 
realistic skin texture around eyes, intimate and emotional mood
```

---

### 特写（Close-up, CU）
**描述**：画面主要包含面部或产品主体
**应用**：
- 面部特写：表情、妆容、发型
- 上装特写：领口、袖口、面料
- 配饰特写：项链、耳环、手链

**提示词**：
```
Close-up shot of [面部/上装/配饰], 
[表情描述], [细节描述], 
[光线描述], [氛围关键词]
```

**示例**：
```
Close-up shot of woman's face with gentle smile, 
natural makeup, styled hair, wearing pearl earrings, 
soft diffused lighting, warm and inviting expression, 
portrait photography, realistic skin texture
```

---

### 半身特写（Medium Close-up, MCU）
**描述**：画面包含胸部以上
**应用**：
- 上半身展示：服装上身效果
- 表情与服装结合
- 适合亚马逊主图

**提示词**：
```
Medium close-up of woman from chest up, 
wearing [服装描述], [表情描述], 
[发型描述], [配饰描述], 
[光线描述], [构图描述]
```

**示例**：
```
Medium close-up of professional woman from chest up, 
wearing tailored navy blazer with white blouse, 
confident expression, styled medium-length hair, 
gold necklace, soft natural lighting from side, 
shallow depth of field, editorial fashion photography
```

---

### 中景（Medium Shot, MS）
**描述**：画面包含腰部以上
**应用**：
- 上半身完整展示
- 手势和姿态可见
- 适合展示搭配

**提示词**：
```
Medium shot of woman from waist up, 
wearing [服装描述], [姿态描述], 
[表情描述], [环境描述], 
[光线描述], [氛围关键词]
```

**示例**：
```
Medium shot of woman from waist up in modern office, 
wearing elegant blazer and trousers, 
hands gesturing while speaking, confident expression, 
contemporary workspace in background, natural lighting, 
professional and approachable atmosphere
```

---

### 中远景（Medium Long Shot, MLS）
**描述**：画面包含膝盖以上
**应用**：
- 展示上装和部分下装
- 姿态和动作更完整
- 适合展示整体搭配

**提示词**：
```
Medium long shot of woman from knees up, 
wearing [完整服装描述], [动作描述], 
[表情描述], [环境描述], 
[光线描述], [构图描述]
```

**示例**：
```
Medium long shot of woman from knees up walking in park, 
wearing casual summer dress and sandals, 
relaxed stride, happy expression, 
green trees and path in background, dappled sunlight, 
natural and refreshing atmosphere, lifestyle photography
```

---

### 全身（Long Shot, LS）
**描述**：画面包含完整人物
**应用**：
- 完整服装展示
- 姿态和动作清晰
- 环境氛围强

**提示词**：
```
Full body shot of woman, 
wearing [完整服装描述], [姿态/动作描述], 
[表情描述], [环境描述], 
[光线描述], [构图描述]
```

**示例**：
```
Full body shot of woman standing in city street, 
wearing trendy outfit with jacket and jeans, 
confident pose, looking at camera, 
urban architecture and lights in background, golden hour, 
street style photography, dynamic and fashionable mood
```

---

### 远景（Extreme Long Shot, ELS）
**描述**：人物在环境中较小
**应用**：
- 强调环境氛围
- 人物融入场景
- 适合展示生活方式

**提示词**：
```
Extreme long shot of woman in [环境], 
small figure in vast [landscape/interior], 
wearing [服装描述], [动作描述], 
[环境描述], [光线描述], 
atmospheric and scenic mood
```

**示例**：
```
Extreme long shot of woman walking on beach at sunset, 
small figure against vast ocean and sky, 
wearing flowing dress, carefree stride, 
golden sunset light, waves and sand, 
romantic and dreamy atmosphere, cinematic composition
```

---

## 镜头角度（Camera Angle）

### 平视（Eye Level）
**特点**：最自然、亲切、平等
**应用**：日常场景、对话感、真实感
**提示词**：
```
Eye-level angle, natural perspective, 
direct eye contact with viewer, 
approachable and relatable composition
```

---

### 仰视（Low Angle）
**特点**：显得高大、有气势、权威
**应用**：职业装、晚装、自信形象
**提示词**：
```
Low angle shot looking up at subject, 
powerful and commanding perspective, 
subject appears taller and more imposing, 
dramatic and empowering composition
```

---

### 俯视（High Angle）
**特点**：显得小巧、脆弱、可爱
**应用**：休闲装、可爱风格、亲密感
**提示词**：
```
High angle shot looking down at subject, 
gentle and intimate perspective, 
subject appears smaller and more approachable, 
soft and caring composition
```

---

### 侧面（Side View）
**特点**：展示轮廓、姿态、立体感
**应用**：展示服装剪裁、身材曲线
**提示词**：
```
Side profile view, showing silhouette and posture, 
three-dimensional feel, 
[服装描述] clearly visible from side, 
elegant and sculptural composition
```

---

### 3/4侧面（Three-quarter View）
**特点**：最常用、最自然、立体感强
**应用**：人像摄影、产品展示
**提示词**：
```
Three-quarter view, natural and flattering angle, 
face slightly turned, both eyes visible, 
立体感和深度感强, classic portrait composition
```

---

### 过肩（Over the Shoulder, OTS）
**特点**：增加对话感、故事性
**应用**：双人场景、互动展示
**提示词**：
```
Over the shoulder shot, 
viewing subject from behind another person's shoulder, 
creates sense of connection and story, 
intimate and narrative composition
```

---

## 光影方向（Lighting Direction）

### 正面光（Front Lighting）
**特点**：均匀照亮、减少阴影
**应用**：产品清晰展示、减少瑕疵
**提示词**：
```
Front lighting, even illumination, 
minimal shadows, clear and detailed view, 
product-focused lighting, clean and professional
```

---

### 侧光（Side Lighting）
**特点**：增加立体感、质感、戏剧性
**应用**：突出面料质感、面部轮廓
**提示词**：
```
Side lighting from [left/right], 
creates depth and dimension, 
dramatic shadows on [one side of face/garment], 
sculptural and artistic lighting
```

---

### 逆光（Backlight）
**特点**：轮廓光、梦幻、浪漫
**应用**：浪漫场景、度假装、晚装
**提示词**：
```
Backlighting, subject outlined with soft glow, 
dreamy and romantic atmosphere, 
light creating halo effect around hair, 
ethereal and magical mood
```

---

### 伦勃朗光（Rembrandt Lighting）
**特点**：经典人像光、面部三角光
**应用**：高级人像、艺术感
**提示词**：
```
Rembrandt lighting, triangle of light on cheek, 
dramatic and classic portrait lighting, 
one side of face in shadow, 
artistic and sophisticated mood
```

---

### 蝴蝶光（Butterfly Lighting）
**特点**：时尚人像、蝴蝶形阴影
**应用**：时尚摄影、美妆展示
**提示词**：
```
Butterfly lighting, light directly above and in front, 
small shadow under nose, flattering for faces, 
glamorous and fashion-forward lighting, 
elegant and polished look
```

---

## 光线质感（Light Quality）

### 硬光（Hard Light）
**特点**：边缘清晰、对比强烈
**应用**：戏剧性、时尚感、轮廓强调
**提示词**：
```
Hard lighting, sharp shadows, high contrast, 
dramatic and bold mood, 
defined edges and strong highlights, 
fashion editorial style
```

---

### 柔光（Soft Light）
**特点**：边缘柔和、过渡自然
**应用**：皮肤美化、温馨氛围、产品展示
**提示词**：
```
Soft diffused lighting, gentle shadows, 
smooth transitions, flattering for skin, 
warm and inviting atmosphere, 
natural and authentic feel
```

---

### 自然光（Natural Light）
**特点**：真实、亲切、变化丰富
**应用**：生活方式、户外场景、真实感
**提示词**：
```
Natural light, authentic and realistic, 
[时间描述：morning/afternoon/golden hour], 
[天气描述：sunny/cloudy/dappled], 
genuine and relatable atmosphere
```

---

### 影棚光（Studio Light）
**特点**：可控、专业、干净
**应用**：产品展示、商业摄影、白背景
**提示词**：
```
Studio lighting, controlled and professional, 
even illumination, clean background, 
product-focused, commercial photography style, 
high-end and polished look
```

---

## 构图技巧（Composition）

### 三分法（Rule of Thirds）
**特点**：将画面分为9宫格，主体放在交叉点
**应用**：最常用构图、平衡感好
**提示词**：
```
Rule of thirds composition, 
subject positioned at [left/right] third intersection, 
balanced and dynamic layout, 
natural and pleasing arrangement
```

---

### 中心构图（Center Composition）
**特点**：主体在画面中央，对称感强
**应用**：正式场合、对称场景、产品特写
**提示词**：
```
Center composition, subject in middle of frame, 
symmetrical and formal arrangement, 
direct and focused presentation, 
clean and structured layout
```

---

### 引导线（Leading Lines）
**特点**：利用线条引导视线到主体
**应用**：街道、走廊、路径场景
**提示词**：
```
Leading lines composition, 
[道路/走廊/栏杆] directing eye to subject, 
creates depth and movement, 
dynamic and engaging layout
```

---

### 框架构图（Framing）
**特点**：利用环境元素框住主体
**应用**：门窗、拱门、树枝框架
**提示词**：
```
Natural framing composition, 
subject framed by [doorway/arch/trees], 
creates depth and focus, 
artistic and storytelling layout
```

---

### 负空间（Negative Space）
**特点**：大面积留白，突出主体
**应用**：极简风格、高级感、产品突出
**提示词**：
```
Negative space composition, 
minimal background, subject stands out, 
clean and minimal aesthetic, 
focus on subject, high-end feel
```

---

## 景深控制（Depth of Field）

### 浅景深（Shallow DOF）
**特点**：主体清晰，背景虚化
**应用**：突出主体、人像摄影、产品特写
**提示词**：
```
Shallow depth of field, 
subject in sharp focus, background blurred, 
bokeh effect, creamy background, 
isolation of subject, professional look
```

---

### 深景深（Deep DOF）
**特点**：前后都清晰
**应用**：风景、环境展示、故事性强
**提示词**：
```
Deep depth of field, 
everything in focus from foreground to background, 
sharp details throughout, 
environmental storytelling, scenic view
```

---

### 移轴效果（Tilt-shift）
**特点**：部分区域清晰，其他区域模糊
**应用**：微缩模型感、创意效果
**提示词**：
```
Tilt-shift effect, selective focus, 
[区域] in sharp focus, rest blurred, 
miniature model appearance, 
creative and artistic effect
```

---

## 镜头运动（Camera Movement）

### 推镜（Push In）
**特点**：镜头靠近主体，增加亲密感
**应用**：情感高潮、细节揭示
**提示词**：
```
Camera pushing in towards subject, 
gradually closer, increasing intimacy, 
dramatic and emotional movement, 
focus tightening on [detail/expression]
```

---

### 拉镜（Pull Out）
**特点**：镜头远离主体，揭示环境
**应用**：开场、环境展示、故事结束
**提示词**：
```
Camera pulling out from subject, 
revealing wider environment, 
contextual and storytelling movement, 
subject becomes part of larger scene
```

---

### 摇镜（Pan）
**特点**：镜头水平旋转，展示场景
**应用**：环境介绍、跟随动作
**提示词**：
```
Camera panning [left/right], 
sweeping view of [environment/action], 
smooth and cinematic movement, 
revealing scene gradually
```

---

### 环绕（Orbit）
**特点**：围绕主体旋转，3D感
**应用**：产品展示、人物环绕
**提示词**：
```
Camera orbiting around subject, 
360-degree view, three-dimensional feel, 
showing all angles of [subject/clothing], 
dynamic and engaging movement
```

---

## 组合示例

### 职业装 - 办公场景
```
Medium close-up, eye-level angle, 
soft natural side lighting, 
shallow depth of field with office background blurred, 
three-quarter view, 
professional and confident composition
```

### 休闲装 - 咖啡店场景
```
Medium shot, slightly high angle, 
warm interior lighting with window light, 
medium depth of field, 
relaxed and intimate composition, 
lifestyle photography feel
```

### 度假装 - 海滩场景
```
Full body shot, low angle, 
golden hour backlight, 
shallow depth of field with ocean bokeh, 
center composition, 
romantic and dreamy atmosphere
```

### 晚装 - 高端餐厅场景
```
Medium close-up, eye-level, 
soft candlelight and ambient lighting, 
shallow depth of field, 
three-quarter view, 
elegant and sophisticated composition
```
